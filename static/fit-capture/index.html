Fit capture

How it works
  1. Put the phone on a stand, or hand it to a friend. Check the picture below, then press Start.
  2. Step back until your whole body, head to feet, is in view.
  3. Hold both arms out straight, a little away from your body, like a Christmas tree.
  4. Turn slowly in place through one full circle and finish facing the camera.

The assistant talks you through it, and you can ask it questions at any time. Recording stops on its own.
